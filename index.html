Yes. TGM Trades is built for beginner and intermediate traders.

Trading education, a private community, trading signals, the weekly TGM Watchlist newsletter, live market discussions, the TGM Trades Vault (call recordings and stock market strategy), a buddy from day 1, psychology and risk management, e-books, and Miyyah's personal strategies.
